## Next

`/{{skill}} next` picks for you, among this skill's own type of task (`type: dev` for `/dev`, `type: ops` for `/ops`). Candidates are `backlog` or `planned` tasks, unassigned or yours, no open `needs`, every dep `done`. An umbrella is a candidate only once all its children are done — its verification pass is due (*Umbrella close*) — and then it ranks first; one with open children never is. Then due recurring work (`TASKS recur list --due`), then your own `planned` tasks, then the rest. Propose the top one in a line (title, and why it is first), with the runner-up; on a yes, run it as *Running a task* says (an umbrella goes to its pass). Nothing eligible → say so and point at `/{{skill}} add <task-or-goal>`. What could stop the work — collisions, a flow step that needs a person, fork risk — is not filtered here: you are there to make those calls. Runs already in flight are status's (`shared/routes/status.md`). `/{{skill}} next unattended` is a different choice (`shared/routes/unattended.md`), and it never picks an umbrella.
